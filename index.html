TrustReward Rules

Please follow these rules to keep TrustReward fair and safe.

1. Use One Account

Each user is allowed to maintain only one TrustReward account.

2. Use Real Information

Always provide accurate information when completing tasks, surveys, offers, and withdrawals.

3. Complete Activities Honestly

Complete tasks, surveys, and offers according to their instructions.

4. No Fraudulent Activity

Fraud, fake information, bots, automation, or attempts to manipulate rewards are prohibited.

5. Do Not Abuse Offers

Do not exploit duplicate rewards, bugs, errors, or repeated offer completions.

6. Follow Offer Requirements

Some activities require specific conditions. Failure to meet them may result in no reward.

7. Use Genuine Withdrawal Details

Withdrawal information must be accurate and authorized for use.

8. Do Not Exploit Technical Errors

Report technical errors instead of attempting to use them to obtain incorrect rewards.

9. Account Reviews

TrustReward may review accounts and transactions when suspicious activity is detected.

10. Withdrawal Processing

Withdrawals may take 1 to 3 market days to review and process.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, account information, activity information, and withdrawal information.

2. Account Information

Account information is used to create and manage your TrustReward account.

3. Reward Information

We may record completed activities, earned coins, referrals, and reward transactions.

4. Withdrawal Information

Information submitted for withdrawals is used to process and review your request.

5. Fraud Prevention

Information may be used to detect suspicious activity, fraud, abuse, and multiple-account activity.

6. Third-Party Services

Some activities may be provided by third-party survey, offer, task, or reward providers.

7. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect account information and platform activity.

8. Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

9. Support Messages

Messages sent to TrustReward Support may be stored so we can respond to your questions and requests.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as TrustReward develops.
